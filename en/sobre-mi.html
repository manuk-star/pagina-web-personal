About me

I was born in Puebla, Mexico, and decided to make my home in Guadalajara, Jalisco. I studied Computer Science at the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM) in Mexico City, and I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Web Engineering at the University of Oviedo, Spain.

Before coming to Spain, I worked as a Cloud Infrastructure Engineer at CONTPAQi in Guadalajara. My main responsibilities included keeping the cloud infrastructure of the company's products operational, evaluating new architectures, and identifying cost optimisation opportunities through FinOps practices. I also carried out DevOps tasks aimed at automating and improving deployment processes.

I enjoy football, music, mobile devices, and above all, travelling. What I enjoy most about visiting new places is discovering their culture, meeting their people, and trying their food. Every country has something unique to offer, and there is always something that manages to surprise me.

I chose this master's programme because, although my career has always been rooted in technology infrastructure, I am passionate about learning and expanding my knowledge. I am a big AWS enthusiast — Azure is another story, haha — but I believe you should never stop learning. Software development was also an area I wanted to explore and gain new skills in.

I have travelled extensively around Mexico, and this is my third visit to Europe. This time I decided to go a step further: I did not just want to visit new places, but also to study and experience the educational system and culture of Spain up close.

And in case there was any doubt — as a true Mexican, I love spicy food, banda music, reggaeton, and of course tequila. Some traditions are worth keeping!
